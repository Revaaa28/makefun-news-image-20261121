# Sources — news of 21 November 2026

Every story on the front page was checked against reporting published on
21 November 2026 (wire roundups and day-briefings timestamped that morning,
UTC). The competition's day is Saturday 21 November 2026. Wording follows
reported-news style: claims are attributed to whoever made them, and no
outcome is asserted for an event still in progress.

1. **Russian missiles in Poland.** Two Russian missiles and a suspected
   drone came down in Poland; Warsaw called it a deliberate provocation and
   NATO called it a grave violation of allied airspace, and Poland requested
   NATO support. The same morning brought reports of a major Russian missile
   strike on Kyiv, where three bridges were hit. Sources: One Turn daily
   world briefing, 21 Nov 2026 ("Russian missiles hit Poland; Wall Street
   slumps"); RFE/RL latest-news wire, 21 Nov 2026; AppiSToS world wire,
   21 Nov 2026 (prosecutors investigating the object that fell at Nowy
   Lipowiec).
2. **Shenzhen trilateral summit closes.** Trump, Xi and Putin concluded
   the first-ever trilateral meeting, held on the APEC sidelines in
   Shenzhen, issuing a four-point declaration; analysts quoted in the
   coverage say the meeting offered symbolism but few concrete results.
   Sources: One Turn, 21 Nov 2026; RFE/RL, 21 Nov 2026 ("4-Point Declaration
   Issued After Trump, Xi, And Putin Hold First-Ever Trilateral Meeting").
3. **Wall Street correction.** US stocks fell about 3.7% on Friday,
   pushing the S&P 500 and the Dow into correction territory as AI and chip
   shares sold off; Japan's Nikkei dropped 5.06% and gold fell almost 4% in
   the global rout. Sources: One Turn market wrap, 21 Nov 2026.
4. **Sudan — West Kordofan.** Sudan's army reports that it dismantled RSF
   strongholds in 10 areas of the West Kordofan region. The claim is the
   army's own and is presented as such. Source: global news wire roundup,
   21 Nov 2026 (Binder global news feed).
5. **Malaysia — Rohingya resettlement.** The Malaysian government has
   finalised an agreement with Türkiye to relocate 10,000 Rohingya asylum
   seekers, days after the International Court of Justice found Myanmar
   committed genocide against the Rohingya (ruling reported 19 Nov 2026,
   nine-to-six vote). Sources: global news wire roundup, 21 Nov 2026;
   Daily Jagran school-assembly headlines, 21 Nov 2026; One Turn, 21 Nov
   2026.
6. **Cricket — Wellington.** India's openers Yashasvi Jaiswal and KL Rahul
   shared a century opening stand at Wellington, a new high for Indian
   openers on New Zealand soil. Source: Daily Jagran sports headlines,
   21 Nov 2026.
7. **Ticker — EU aid.** The Council of the EU adopted decisions for
   €1 billion in military aid to Ukraine through the European Peace
   Facility. Source: AppiSToS world wire, 20–21 Nov 2026.
8. **Ticker — Nigeria barge.** Nigerian rescue teams are searching for
   survivors after a funeral barge overturned in Bayelsa. Source: global
   news wire roundup, 21 Nov 2026.
9. **Ticker — cancer pill.** A pill has been hailed as a breakthrough for
   pancreatic cancer. Source: One Turn, 21 Nov 2026.
